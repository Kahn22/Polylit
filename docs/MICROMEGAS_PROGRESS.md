## Source record

- Selected base: Voltaire, *Micromégas*, *Œuvres complètes*, Garnier frères, Paris, 1877, volume 21, printed pages 105–122. Seven chapters, complete work. The BnF 1752 catalogue item is a separate bibliographic witness; its readings are not merged into this text.
- `content/sources/wrk_voltaire_micromegas/` holds the frozen HTML and visible transcription, 18 individual page transcriptions, 18 historical scan images, a seven-chapter candidate text, hashes, page mapping and mechanical comparisons. Scans and nineteenth-century editorial notes are source evidence, not learner content.
- The candidate has 33 source paragraphs and 6,608 word tokens under the candidate counting rule. All 6,608 compare in order to the saved page transcriptions after excluding the seven headings from prose (they remain navigation metadata), removing 34 editorial note calls, joining two words split across printed page breaks and applying documented printed-reading decisions. The text ends with the final sentence of chapter VII. See `page-comparison.json`.
- All 18 page scans were visually inspected and the seven chapters visually proofread against the selected edition. The print has `au delà` on pp. 107–108 where the online transcription hyphenates it; an apparent stray printer hyphen in `tous- les êtres` on p. 109 is corrected to `tous les êtres`; chapter IV restores `quelque apparence` and `fût` from pp. 113–114; chapters VI–VII restore the unaccented `O` vocatives, missing `lui`, and abbreviated `M.` on pp. 118 and 121. Rough OCR against each page transcription averages 0.886 token alignment; this is a supporting error search, **not** a proof that every printed letter matches. `canonical.txt` version 1 is now source approved, byte-identical to the reviewed draft. This decision relies on the recorded visual proofreading and documented exceptions, not the mechanical result alone. It does not approve learner vocabulary, expressions, or questions.
- Original French literary prose was assessed for US and French public-domain reuse using US Copyright Office Circular 15A, French Code L123-1 and the BnF author/edition records. Translations, editorial notes and independently protected images remain excluded.
